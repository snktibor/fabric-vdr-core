The frozen bridge proves the service-trace-to-atomic-ledger composition conditionally on explicit Java Hoare, codec/runtime, platform and fidelity boundaries. It contains 12 exact target proofs, 18 auxiliary proofs and 15 witness theorems. No existing model was changed. The authoritative fresh status, hashes and counts are in [result.json](result.json); theorem dependencies are in [theorem-inventory.json](theorem-inventory.json) and [logs/axioms.log](logs/axioms.log).

The raw interpreter checks only normal return, external permission and captured read revisions. It installs the immutable write images and atomic extra writes. It never tests `eligible`. The proofs derive eligibility from actual observed optional records, guard version comparisons and bounded target arithmetic, derive exact equality with `writtenRecord`, establish read coverage and revision equality, then prove apply/step/run/commit correspondence to the existing Atomic model. All occurrence indexes remain intact, including rejects and creates. Snapshot origins are transported from the raw run. Creation cannot overwrite a present target on acceptance.

The honest-check bridge transports a present record with its accumulator and version at the declared check prefix, and the guards' pins at that same prefix. The two parties keep independent prefixes. State, commit and revocation-history correspondence feed the existing asynchronous no-bifurcation theorem. `EndToEndSpec.realizes` denotes an authenticated payload's intended semantic effect. It does not assert that both transactions durably revoke: the conclusion rules out both committing.

The nonvacuity witnesses are universally parameterized by the explicit injective `Encoding` boundary; this artifact does not construct or verify the production String/composite-key codec. Their service postconditions and snapshot relations are proved without an eligibility premise. The full-record examples exhibit accumulator/version provenance, and the nonce witness demonstrates that extra conflicts can reject despite matching entry reads. These are semantic service traces, not executions of Java/Fabric or cryptographic checks.

The principal remaining obligation is a matching complete external Hoare/runtime package for `PerCallContracts`. Normal-case requires describe sufficient success conditions; they are not silently inverted into an unconditional characterization of normal returns. The public exceptional partition and supported typed/runtime domain must justify that implication. The [premise ledger](JML-PREMISES.md) identifies each required clause and every still-external adapter. It also records the signer-domain correction: S3's nonblank owner alone does not imply trim-nonempty; inspected `JwtValidator.requireText`/`JsonUtil.trimToNull` and the caller binding supply the narrower source-supported domain, still conditional on runtime correspondence.

Actual deployment ordering, peer-prefix provenance, MVCC read registration, rank/no-ABA interpretation and whole-proposal rollback remain platform premises. Correct credential identities, check/pin provenance, authenticated issuer behavior and payload/revocation fidelity remain client/cryptographic premises. The proof does not verify Fabric, establish JWT security, certify accumulator arithmetic or reproduce the original Argos artifact.
