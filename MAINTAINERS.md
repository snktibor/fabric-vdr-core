## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are added by consensus of the existing maintainers, based on a
sustained record of high-quality contributions and reviews. Open an issue if you
are interested.

## Maintainer responsibilities

- Review and merge pull requests (DCO sign-off and green CI are prerequisites,
  see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Triage issues and security reports ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
- Uphold the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
